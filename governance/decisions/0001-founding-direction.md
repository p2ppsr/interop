# Decision 0001 — establishment of TPNIL

Adopted: 2026-09-17 UTC. Authority: PEER TO PEER PRIVACY SYSTEMS RESEARCH LLC, founding steward.

The founding steward establishes the Transaction Processing Network Interoperability Laboratory under the following terms:

1. The laboratory operates through `p2ppsr/interop`, private during establishment. Public release requires a separate recorded decision.
2. The founding steward holds initial authority under the [governance doctrine](../../GOVERNANCE.md), with meritocratic maintainer responsibility, rough-consensus technical deliberation, and provisional multi-stakeholder transition paths. The laboratory maintains an open offer to explore BSVA stewardship and is currently unaffiliated with BSVA.
3. The [founding-participation register](../../GOVERNANCE.md#founding-participation-and-affiliations) records P2PPSR and Ty Everett as accepted participants sharing one affiliation vote, and standing offers to the remaining named parties. The provisional council has standing authority to activate under the published participation, independence, and transition conditions.
4. All original laboratory code, documentation, vectors, criteria, specifications, infrastructure, reference implementations, and evaluators use the [Open BSV licensing policy](../../LICENSE.md).
5. Contribution terms provide controlled-IP and patent grants and nonassertion for permitted BSV uses, including migration or import into BSV and basic swaps with BSV as an actual source or destination leg.
6. Proprietary SUTs retain their ownership and licensing, subject to perpetual worldwide canonical-artifact archival and evaluation grants.
7. Initial findings are experimental. Certification requires the independence, evidence, and appeals conditions in governance.
8. Controlled fixtures use D0 byte replay. Native SUTs may use D1 semantic determinism under explicit vector criteria.
9. ISI-1 defines full-stack integration; ISI-2 overlay interoperability; ISI-3 Remote Wallet Storage interoperability; and ISI-4 BRC-100 wallet/application interoperability. Each begins at version 0.1.0 and derives from ITCVE-1.
10. BSV Desktop is the initial reference wallet candidate. `p2ppsr/todo-ts` supplies the initial application candidate. Both follow the common admission requirements.
11. The laboratory's contribution and artifact agreements adopt Oregon law and the dispute forum specified in [governance](../../GOVERNANCE.md#governing-law-and-dispute-forum).

Implementation priorities and outstanding technical decisions are maintained in the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) and [decision register](../../docs/decisions.md).
