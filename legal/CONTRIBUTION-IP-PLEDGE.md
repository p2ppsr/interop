# TPNIL contribution agreement and BSV IP pledge

Version 0.1.0. This agreement takes effect for a Contributor through the acceptance record defined below. Original laboratory materials are licensed under [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md).

## 1. Parties, acceptance and subject matter

The **Contributor** is the natural person or entity identified in an executed acceptance record. The **Steward** is PEER TO PEER PRIVACY SYSTEMS RESEARCH LLC, an Oregon limited liability company identified in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md). **Beneficiaries** include the Steward, current and future laboratory participants, downstream recipients and implementers exercising a Permitted BSV Use.

An acceptance record identifies the agreement/schedule versions and SHA-256 digests, contributor legal name, representative and authority, employer/affiliate rights as applicable, date, contribution commit/object digests, disclosed third-party components and an affirmative signature or other legally effective assent. Participation, naming in governance, opening an issue, repository access, and silence are not substitutes for that assent. Contributions accepted into the normative corpus require a traceable valid rights record.

**Contribution** includes intentionally submitted original code, documentation, requirements, vectors, fixtures, evaluation methods, ISI/ITCVE definitions, reference implementation code and other identified material, together with revisions expressly within the acceptance. A SUT binary submitted only for evaluation remains outside this definition and uses the artifact agreement.

## 2. Controlled rights

**Controlled Rights** are copyright and related rights, database rights, patent rights and applications, design rights, trade-secret permissions in intentionally disclosed material, and other licensable IP that the Contributor owns or has authority to license without violating another obligation. **Covered Patent Claims** include present and future claims controlled by the Contributor that are practiced by the Contribution as supplied or are necessarily practiced to implement its contributed normative requirements, vectors or evaluation methods, including the required elements of optional features when implemented. This covers standards implementation in independently written software, not just copying reference code.

Controlled Rights exclude third-party rights outside the Contributor's licensing authority and patents unrelated to the defined Contribution. The agreement requires no disclosure of unrelated confidential information or exhaustive patent search. Claims necessary to implement contributed requirements remain covered regardless of their designation as background IP. The Contributor shall disclose known relevant encumbrances and obtain necessary employer and affiliate approvals before submission.

## 3. Express grants

For every Permitted BSV Use in the accepted PERMITTED-USE schedule, Contributor grants Beneficiaries a worldwide, non-exclusive, fully paid, royalty-free license for the full duration of the Controlled Rights, including applicable renewals/extensions, to:

- use, reproduce, analyze, implement, execute, adapt, modify, create derivatives of, combine, publicly display/perform, distribute, publish, host, archive, sublicense and commercialize the Contribution under LICENSE.md;
- under Covered Patent Claims, make, have made, use, offer for sale, sell, supply, import, export and otherwise practice the Contribution and conforming implementations of the contributed requirements, including independent implementations;
- extract and reuse protected databases and exercise other Controlled Rights necessary for those permitted acts.

These grants attach to accepted contributions and are irrevocable for covered compliant uses; later withdrawal from governance, removal of a contribution from a future release, acquisition, insolvency to the extent applicable law permits, or a later policy version does not rescind an already granted license. No fee, contributor membership, use of a specific implementation or certification purchase is required to exercise them. Redistribution shall preserve applicable notices and field-of-use terms.

## 4. Covenant, waiver and moral rights

Contributor covenants not to assert Controlled Rights against any Beneficiary for a covered Permitted BSV Use. To the extent legally permitted, Contributor waives remedies and claims based on those rights for that use. Where waiver is ineffective or rights are inalienable, Contributor grants the broadest lawful license/consent and covenants not to enforce them against that use. To the extent permitted, Contributor consents to modification, attribution practices, integration and publication and waives or agrees not to assert moral/similar rights in a manner that would block the grant. Required credit and accurate provenance remain preserved.

This is a BSV-field license and nonassertion commitment, **not** abandonment into the public domain. Controlled Rights outside the permitted field remain reserved. A compliant BSV-only implementation cannot infringe the Contributor's Covered Patent Claims or other Controlled Rights by performing the licensed acts; this is not a guarantee concerning unrelated patents, noncontributors, other conduct or mandatory law.

## 5. Transfers and later-acquired rights

Contributor must disclose this grant to any transferee of affected Controlled Rights and require a written undertaking to preserve it to the extent legally possible. No transfer is intended to strip existing Beneficiaries of granted permissions. Later-acquired claims fitting the Covered Patent Claims definition are included when Contributor has authority to grant them. The Contributor shall execute confirmations or recordations required by applicable law to give effect to these obligations.

## 6. Eligibility, third-party material and remedies

Contributor represents authority to grant the identified rights, intentional submission, and disclosure of known conflicting obligations. Contributions shall preserve third-party licenses and notices and carry permissions compatible with the required laboratory grants. Third-party patent permissions require the rights holder's grant. Disputed contributions are suspended from new normative reliance while the laboratory preserves the evidence and records a resolution.

The laboratory does not accept unilateral opt-outs from rights necessary to implement an adopted contributed requirement. Adoption requires removal or revision of incompatible material, or a compatible grant. This rule does not prohibit proprietary SUT submissions under their separate agreement.

Out-of-scope uses receive no permission under this agreement. A breach concerning such a use does not retroactively revoke an unrelated Beneficiary's protected compliant use. Additional notice, cure, injunctive, damages, fee-shifting, or patent-retaliation terms require express agreement consistent with the irrevocable covered-use grants.

No trademark endorsement, certification mark, agency, partnership, employment or membership is granted. Contributions are provided without warranty to the extent permitted by the adopted Open BSV terms; authority representations and express grants remain as stated. The agreement remains subject to mandatory law, including applicable capacity, consumer, competition, standards, and insolvency rules.

The [governing-law and dispute-forum clause](../GOVERNANCE.md#governing-law-and-dispute-forum) is incorporated into this agreement at the version and digest identified in the acceptance record. That record includes notice details, representative authority, required execution formalities, and any institutional successor accession. Where that is impossible, affected admissions are suspended pending a recorded resolution.
