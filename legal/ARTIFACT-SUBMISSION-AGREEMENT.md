# Canonical SUT artifact submission agreement

Version 1.0.0. This agreement takes effect through the signed submission schedule described in section 5. The Steward is PEER TO PEER PRIVACY SYSTEMS RESEARCH LLC, identified in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md).

## 1. Ownership and admitted subject

The Submitter retains all ownership of its SUT, binaries, source, patents and marks except the express licenses granted here. Proprietary SUTs may participate and need not be relicensed under the Open BSV License. The submission schedule identifies original artifact bytes/digests, platform/signature metadata, release/source identifiers where disclosed, dependencies, configuration, authorized reference use, contact and rights holder.

No license to unrelated source code or commercial product functions is required. An evaluation adapter contributed as laboratory infrastructure is separately subject to the laboratory contribution policy. A submitted SUT designated as a comparison/reference **candidate** remains a SUT; a laboratory-authored reference implementation is Covered Material. Reference designation preserves the artifact owner's licensing terms.

## 2. Perpetual worldwide laboratory license

Submitter grants the Steward and authorized laboratory successors, operators, archival custodians, mirrors and independent evaluators a non-exclusive, royalty-free, fully paid, worldwide license for the duration of all applicable rights, including renewals/extensions, to receive, copy, preserve, install, execute, inspect observable behavior of, test, benchmark, verify, host and redistribute the **canonical submitted artifacts** for BSV interoperability evaluation, reproducibility, educational explanation, audit, appeals and historical archival access.

The grant expressly includes indefinite hosting of the exact submitted binaries in public or access-controlled content-addressed archives and delivery to independent evaluators globally, subject to mandatory law. The submitter grants relevant controlled copyright, patent, database and other permissions necessary for those acts. It authorizes publication of scoped test results, interoperability findings, and necessary evidence/excerpts without prior favorable-result approval. It does not transfer title or require disclosure of source code, trade secrets not exposed by ordinary testing, or product signing keys.

This grant is irrevocable for admitted artifacts and survives withdrawal from a program, superseding releases, governance changes or a commercial license expiry, subject to mandatory law. Downstream laboratory archival/evaluation permissions already granted are preserved. Commercial redistribution for unrelated purposes is not authorized by this agreement.

## 3. Practical replay rights

Submitter warrants authority to grant the above license and discloses dependencies, embedded third-party assets, redistribution limits, activation requirements, device entitlement limits, expiration and technical protection that could prevent lawful continued evaluation. The Submitter shall supply lawful offline evaluation entitlements and installation materials as needed. The agreement does not authorize bypassing DRM, platform security or third-party terms.

Admission requires worldwide archival and replay rights for dependencies, or replacements carrying those rights. The submission schedule shall address provisioning expiration, license-server dependencies, and other replay constraints. Candidate status applies until these requirements are met. Reports disclose platform replay limits while preserving archived evidence.

## 4. Evidence and confidentiality

Submissions shall use isolated laboratory identities and the selected profile’s funding fixtures and exclude customer information, real credentials, and unrelated confidential data. The Submitter shall identify restricted material before transfer. Public conformance results and canonical artifact retention cannot be vetoed merely because a result is unfavorable. Any confidentiality schedule must preserve independent review and the stated reproducibility claim; if it cannot, the run must be labeled restricted/experimental and cannot support public certification.

The lab may delay publication under a published defect-handling process; it must retain the original evidence and a record of the reason. Corrected binaries create new identities and new runs. The submitter can contest errors through the appeal process; it cannot silently replace archived bytes.

## 5. Succession, limits and acceptance

The Steward may transfer archival and evaluation responsibilities to an institutional successor that assumes these duties in writing. The transfer and acceptance shall be announced and recorded.

Submission grants no favorable result, certification, exclusive access, governance seat or endorsement. The laboratory shall preserve submitter attribution and accurate version and digest labels. Nothing guarantees universal noninfringement or overrides export/sanctions restrictions or mandatory legal obligations. Rights cannot be granted on behalf of nonparties.

The [governing-law and dispute-forum clause](../GOVERNANCE.md#governing-law-and-dispute-forum) is incorporated into this agreement at the version and digest identified in the submission schedule. Admission requires a signed schedule identifying verified legal identities and representative authority, artifact digests, accepted agreement digest, effective date, retention and public-access settings, lawful replay entitlements, notice details, and the incorporated governance provisions.
